BNE Sales — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

BNE Sales ("the app") is an internal field-sales and team tool used by employees of BN Enterprises ("BNE") and its authorised team members. It is not offered to the general public. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Who can use the app

Only people registered by BNE's administrator can sign in, using their company email address and a one-time code sent to that email. Accounts are created and disabled by the company.

Data we collect

  • Account and profile: name, work email, phone number, designation, role, branch, territory, vehicle type for fuel reimbursement, and an optional profile photo.
  • Precise location while on duty: from the moment a salesperson or manager starts the work day (tapping Start Day or making the first customer check-in) until End Day or the company's automatic end time, the app records the device's GPS location every few minutes (normally every 10 minutes), together with the battery level and whether a mock-location app is in use. This continues while the app is closed or not in use, and an "On duty" notification is shown the whole time. Location is also recorded at each customer check-in and check-out. No location is recorded outside duty hours. Users are told about this and must accept it in the app before location access is requested.
  • Business records entered by staff: leads and customers (names, company, phone, email, address, requirements), visits, quotations, deal values, purchase orders, billing requests and payment details (amount, date, mode, UTR/reference).
  • Photos and files: visiting cards, site photos, drawings, purchase orders, payment screenshots and chat attachments. Site photos are stamped with date, time and GPS. Text on visiting cards is read on the device to pre-fill contact fields.
  • Team chat: messages, reactions, mentions and attachments sent in the app's internal chat.
  • Device push token to deliver work notifications (new leads, quote requests, messages).

The app does not collect contacts from your phone, does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

Why we use it

  • To verify customer visits and show managers where the field team is during working hours.
  • To calculate the distance travelled each day for fuel reimbursement.
  • To manage leads, quotations, orders, payment collection, sales targets and incentives.
  • To let team members communicate with each other.

Who can see it

Data is visible only to the user concerned and, according to their role, to BNE's branch managers, heads and administrators. It is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Data is processed by our service providers on BNE's behalf: Supabase (database, storage and sign-in; hosted in India – Mumbai region), Google Firebase (push notifications), Google Maps Platform (maps, addresses and road distance) and Resend (sign-in and work emails). All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Retention and deletion

Business records are kept for as long as required for business, tax and accounting purposes. Location points are kept for up to 24 months for reimbursement audits and then deleted. When someone leaves the company their access is disabled.

You can ask for your account and personal data to be deleted by emailing manu@bne.org.in from your registered email. We will delete your profile, photos, chat messages and location history within 30 days, except records we are legally required to keep (for example invoices and payment entries), which are retained without your profile details.

Your choices

Location is recorded only while you are on duty and stops at End Day. You can revoke the app's location or notification permission at any time in your phone settings; this stops duty tracking and may affect visit verification and fuel claims.

Children

The app is for employees only and is not directed at anyone under 18.
