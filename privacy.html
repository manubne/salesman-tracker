BNE Sales — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

BNE Sales ("the app") is an internal field-sales tool used by employees of BNE and its authorised team members. It is not offered to the general public. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Who can use the app

Only people invited by BNE's administrator (salespeople, coordinators, tele-callers, quotation staff and managers) can sign in, using their registered mobile number.

Data we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, role, territory, and vehicle type for fuel reimbursement.
  • Location while on duty: when a salesperson taps Start Day, the app records the phone's GPS location every few minutes (the interval is set by the company, normally 10 minutes) together with the battery level, until End Day or the company's auto end time. A persistent notification is shown the whole time. No location is recorded outside duty hours. Location is also recorded at the moment of a customer check-in and check-out.
  • Customer and lead information entered by staff: names, company, phone, email, address, requirements, deal values, purchase-order and payment details.
  • Photos taken in the app: visiting cards, site photos, drawings, purchase orders and payment proofs. Site photos are stamped with date, time and GPS.
  • Device push token to deliver work notifications (new leads, quote requests).

Why we use it

  • To verify customer visits and show managers where the field team is during working hours.
  • To calculate the distance travelled each day for fuel reimbursement.
  • To manage leads, quotations, orders and sales targets/incentives.

Sharing

Data is visible only to the salesperson concerned and to BNE's managers and administrators. It is not sold or shared with advertisers. Data is stored with our cloud providers (Supabase, hosted in India – Mumbai region; Google Firebase for notifications; Google Maps Platform to calculate road distance from location points).

Retention

Business records are kept for as long as required for business and accounting purposes. Location points are kept for up to 24 months for reimbursement audits and then deleted. On leaving the company, a user's access is disabled.

Your choices

Location tracking runs only after you tap Start Day and stops at End Day. You can revoke the app's location or notification permission at any time in your phone settings; this will stop duty tracking and may affect visit verification and fuel claims.

Contact

For questions or to request access to or correction of your data, contact BNE at manu@bne.org.in.
