BNE Sales — Delete your account or data

App: BNE Sales (Android) · Developer: BN Enterprises

BNE Sales is the internal field-sales app of BN Enterprises. You can ask us to delete your whole account, or only some of your data, at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to manu@bne.org.in from the email address you use to sign in to BNE Sales.
  2. Use the subject "BNE Sales – delete my account" (or "delete my data" and say which data, for example location history or chat messages).
  3. We confirm receipt within 3 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days. You will get an email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, designation and profile photo
  • Your location history recorded while on duty
  • Your chat messages, reactions and chat attachments
  • Photos you uploaded (site photos, visiting cards) that are not part of a company record we must keep
  • Your sign-in account and device notification token

What is kept, and for how long

  • Business records that the company is legally required to keep for tax and accounting — invoices, orders, quotations and payment entries — are kept for up to 8 years, with your name and contact details removed from them.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days after deletion.

See also our privacy policy. Contact: BN Enterprises · manu@bne.org.in · bne.org.in
