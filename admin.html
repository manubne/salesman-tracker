OEM access manufacturer people who can only check stock in the app

An OEM user signs in to the BNEOne app with this mobile number (SMS code) or email (emailed code) and sees one screen: Stock check. They cannot see customers, prices, invoices, service jobs or anything else. Switch a person off to sign them out immediately.

App version force everyone onto a new release

Build = the last number of the version, e.g. 1.0.160 → 160. Set the minimum only after the new build is live on the Play Store for everyone, otherwise people cannot update yet. 0 = no block.
